OUTREACH

Ongoing Outreach

Beyond its signature events, the Council carries out long-term dissemination and industry-empowerment work. On one hand, together with academic platforms at home and abroad, it compiles and publishes clinical case records of overseas Chinese medicine, supports the editing and publication of professional journals such as Overseas Chinese Medicine, documents the historical record of overseas Chinese medicine, and promotes the achievements of overseas masters--providing a valuable venue for academic publication and professional exchange for overseas TCM talent. On the other hand, working with local Chinese-Canadian community organizations and medical institutions, it regularly organizes small academic salons, public health lectures, and community exercise sessions, bringing traditional practices such as Tai Chi and Baduanjin to overseas communities.
