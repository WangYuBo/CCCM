EVALUATION

Industry Evaluation System

Notably, in the course of advancing overseas Chinese medicine, the Council and the industry forces behind it have also addressed the pressing pain points of overseas practitioners. Because overseas TCM practitioners long lacked any unified body to handle professional qualification recognition, continuing education, professional-title evaluation, and practice-rights protection, the Council took the lead, together with allied forces, in founding the Overseas Masters and Notables of Chinese Medicine Evaluation Committee. On the 96th Chinese Medicine Day, the Committee publicly announced the inaugural list of "Overseas Masters of Chinese Medicine" and "Overseas Notables of Chinese Medicine." The move filled a gap in the overseas TCM evaluation system and gave overseas practitioners a solid professional backbone.

In August 2025, after one month of public notice, the Evaluation Committee announced the second-batch list of Overseas Masters and Notables of Chinese Medicine.
