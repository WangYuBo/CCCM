OVERVIEW

Overview

The International Council of Conference on Health-Care with Chinese Medicine (CCCM) is an international, non-profit TCM industry organization registered in Canada under federal law. Drawing on the professional strength of North America's Chinese-medicine community, the Council has built a transnational, cross-cultural platform for exchange in Chinese medicine. As the exclusive executing organization of such academic research programs as the International TCM AI Innovation Center, the National Masters of Chinese Medicine Forum, the Overseas Chinese Medicine Forum, the Qihuang Literature Month, and the Worldwide Journey of Chinese Medicine, the Council works worldwide to promote Chinese wellness culture, international research on TCM and AI innovation, academic exchange in acupuncture and moxibustion, and public-health services. It has become a core civil-society vehicle for the spread and development of Chinese medicine in Europe and North America.
